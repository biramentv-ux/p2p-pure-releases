Features

Everything a messenger does.
Minus the middleman.

End-to-end encrypted

Every message gets a fresh key (Double Ratchet). Even if someone gets hold of one key, older and later messages stay locked.

No sign-up

A passkey or password on your device. No email, no phone, no SMS code.

Connect with a QR

Show the code, the other person scans it. Or a short 10-character code that lasts 10 minutes.

Files up to 100 MB

Video, documents, music with a built-in player. Paste a picture with Ctrl+V and it is ready to send.

Audio and video calls

Over the same direct link. The media is encrypted (DTLS-SRTP).

Groups of up to 9

Admin, group name and photo, GIFs. A new member does not see old history, and a removed one loses access even while offline.

12 words and you are back

Write them down once. On a new device they bring your profile back.

Move to a new device

Scan a QR from the old phone and your profile moves over, encrypted.

How it works

Three steps.
Zero accounts.

  1. 1

    Create a profile

    One tap. Keys are made on your device and never leave it. You write down the 12 words.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Show the QR code or send a short code. The link between the two devices is direct (WebRTC).

  3. 3

    Compare the words

    You both see the same words. If they match, nobody is in the middle. Done, start talking.

Security, without the boasting

We say what we do.
And what we do not yet.

Signed releases

Each release has a signed list of dependencies (SBOM) and checksums.

Reproducible builds

Two clean builds from the same code give identical files. It is checked automatically.

Checked against a reference

The Double Ratchet passes tests against an independent reference implementation. A paid external audit does not exist yet, and we say so.

The signalling server is blind

In “room” mode a small Worker only relays signed invitations for 10 minutes. It sees no messages and keeps no logs. QR mode uses no server at all.

Encrypted on disk too

History and contacts are stored on the device, encrypted with AES-GCM. The key never leaves it.

An honest warning

The link is direct, so some mobile networks block it. Then make the first connection over one Wi‑Fi network, or through your own TURN server.

FAQ

People ask.
We answer plainly.

Is there really no server?

Not for messages and files. They travel directly. A small server is used only for “rooms”, when you are far apart and cannot scan a QR: it relays the signed invitation for 10 minutes and that is all. Public STUN servers help the two devices find each other without seeing any content.

What if I lose my phone?

The 12 words restore your profile on a new device. So write them on paper. Without them nobody can restore it, including us.

Do we both need to be online?

Yes. With no server to hold messages, they are delivered while you are both connected. That is the price of nobody keeping them for you.

Why does it sometimes not connect?

Some mobile networks block direct links. Try one Wi‑Fi network for the first connection, or add your own TURN server in the settings. We use nobody else's server by default.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. No ads, no subscription, no account to sell.

Is it safe to download an .exe or .apk from here?

The files are built automatically on GitHub from the source code. Each has a SHA-256 checksum, and releases carry a signed SBOM. If the checksum matches, the file is exactly what was built.
